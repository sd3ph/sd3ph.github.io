Physics meets AI

Physics gives us a language for how systems evolve. AI gives us new ways to find patterns and build predictive models. Together, we aim to reveal mechanisms, test hypotheses and support discovery, not replace scientific reasoning with black-box prediction.

Research & competencies

Research that connects disorder to design

Domain expertise in collective intelligence in living and artificial systems, the physics of complex fluids and soft condensed matter. Core competencies: creative research, out-of-equilibrium statistical physics and machine learning of order and disorder.

Soft, glassy, active & adaptable matter

Transitions between fluid-like and rigid states in disordered systems.

Physics of flow, glass & living machines

How driven and active systems flow, jam and reorganise.

Material failure & molecular information processing

How molecular systems store, manipulate and relay information.

Mechanobiology, catastrophe science & emergent intelligence

From tissue dynamics to critical cascades.

Research within the group

We combine large-scale simulation on high-performance computing with statistical methods to build data-driven yet explainable techniques for autonomous organisation and failure in living and artificial systems.

Direction 1

Molecular information processing

Decoding how molecular systems store and relay information, to engineer more responsive artificial systems.

Direction 2

Cellular migration & turbulence

Collective dynamics of migrating cells in tissue formation, repair and failure.

Direction 3

Failure & jamming of amorphous systems

Predictive models of resilience, critical points and jamming in disordered materials.

Direction 4

Predicting vulnerability & avalanches

Critical cascades, from natural avalanches to interconnected economies, and the impact of systemic failure.

Direction 5

Autonomous & critical phenomena

Self-organised behaviour at critical thresholds, and the balance of stability and adaptability.

Direction 6

Generative physical intelligence

Generative models integrated with physical laws, able to produce physically plausible dynamics and structures.

Lab 360° · Science of Complexity

Complexity never sleeps. Neither does Lab 360°.

From quantum materials at midnight to cities at rush hour, complex systems share one language: many interacting parts, emergent behaviour, hidden structure. Lab 360° learns that language with physics and AI, round the clock, and points it at the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

About the lab

Lab 360° is an interdisciplinary research and learning initiative at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore. It connects fundamental physics, artificial intelligence and data science to understand and model complex natural and artificial systems.

The group grew out of work on soft, glassy and active matter, where disorder and collective motion produce behaviour no single particle shows. The same ideas now drive inverse learning of complex systems, Koopman and dynamic-mode methods for prediction, and AI-assisted tools for materials, health and the environment.
