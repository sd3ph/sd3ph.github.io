Lab 360°: Physics, Artificial Intelligence & Data Science Research

Lab 360° — Physics + AI is an interdisciplinary research and learning initiative associated with Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore. It connects fundamental physics, artificial intelligence, machine learning and data science to understand and model complex natural and artificial systems.

Research themes include computational and statistical physics, non-equilibrium and soft matter, complex systems, data-driven modelling, scientific machine learning, materials informatics, molecular dynamics, interpretable AI and high-performance computing. The site also shares research directions, publications, teaching resources, projects and opportunities for collaboration.
