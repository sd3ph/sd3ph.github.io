MATTER / MOTION / LIFE
01 / BIOLOGICAL & COMPLEX SYSTEMS

Physics of Life

How do physical principles shape living systems, from molecules and cells to tissues, organisms and collective behaviour? We explore life as a dynamic, multiscale process—where active matter, fluctuations, transport, mechanics and self-organisation generate structure, adaptation and emergent function.

Our research spans biological and active matter, cellular and tissue mechanics, collective motion, intracellular transport, non-equilibrium dynamics and the physics of living fluids. Models of jamming, yielding, glassy relaxation and non-Gaussian transport help reveal how living and life-inspired materials reorganise, adapt and respond to stress across scales.

02 / COMPUTATIONAL MATERIALS INNOVATION

Material Engineering

We combine materials science, computational modelling and data-driven methods to understand, design and optimise materials for real-world applications. By connecting experimental measurements, scientific literature, simulation outputs and materials databases through integrated data pipelines and APIs, we turn complex information into useful structure–property insights.

Our development directions include materials informatics, property prediction, candidate screening, microstructure analysis, process–structure–property relationships and inverse materials design. Machine learning, physics-informed models and simulation-assisted workflows can accelerate the exploration of functional, lightweight, durable and sustainable materials, while supporting more efficient experimentation and engineering decisions.

03 / HUMAN-CENTRED INTELLIGENCE

Healthcare AI

We develop responsible AI models for personalised wellbeing, functional assessment and accessible screening. Multimodal data—from images and movement to lifestyle and physiological signals—can help characterise individual patterns and support tailored insights, with careful attention to validation, uncertainty, privacy and clinical boundaries.

Development directions include obesity and nutrition assessment, vision-guided neuro-rehabilitation, vision-based functional measurements and screening tools for mental wellbeing. Vision-language models can support more natural therapy guidance and human–AI interaction, helping translate observations into understandable feedback for individuals, caregivers and professionals.

04 / HUMAN-CENTRED INNOVATION

AI for Society

We engineer AI systems that connect data, people and physical infrastructure to address challenges at the scale of communities. Large public datasets, health and environmental APIs, sensor streams and local knowledge can be integrated into automated pipelines for monitoring, forecasting and decision support. In areas such as epidemics and public health, these systems can support epidemic modelling and forecasting, trend analysis, risk mapping, resource coordination and early-warning workflows—helping institutions act on changing conditions with greater speed and situational awareness.

Our work also explores Physical AI: intelligent systems that perceive and act in the real world through cameras, edge devices, mobile platforms and assistive robotics. Applications include vision-guided elderly care, activity and fall-risk awareness, rehabilitation support, assistive technologies and an E-Blood Bank platform for donor discovery and coordination. By combining predictive models, AI-driven automation and human-centred design, we aim to build practical systems for healthcare access, disaster response and resilient public services, with privacy, reliability and human oversight built into deployment.
